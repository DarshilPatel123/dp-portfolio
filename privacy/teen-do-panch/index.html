TEEN DO PANCH

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy describes the current offline, ad-free version of Teen Do Panch. It will be reviewed before a future release changes the game's data practices.

This Privacy Policy describes how Silent Gaming handles information in the Teen Do Panch mobile game.

Information collection

Teen Do Panch is an offline single-player game. The current version does not create user accounts and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or device data.

Advertising, analytics, and online services

The current version does not include:

  • Advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • Analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • Social login, cloud storage, or online multiplayer.
  • Cookies or tracking technologies within the game.

Local game information

Your selected options, completed-match statistics, and any match deliberately saved with Save & Exit are stored only on your device. Statistics and the saved match are kept as separate local records. This local data is used solely to restore preferences, statistics, and the saved match; it is never transmitted off the device.

Data security

Local game information is stored in the app's private storage and is not transmitted. Android cloud backup is disabled for this release. Because the game does not access or collect personal or sensitive user data, no such data is stored or transferred by Silent Gaming.

Retention and deletion

Reset Stats removes only completed-match statistics and does not change a saved match. The last random match identifier may remain solely to prevent duplicate counting after an interrupted file deletion; it contains no account, device, time, or personal information and cannot restore deleted statistics. Abandoning or replacing a saved match removes only that match and does not change statistics. Uninstalling the app removes its remaining local data under normal Android behavior. There is no server-held personal data to review or delete.

Permissions

The game does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or other sensitive information.

Children's privacy

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before any release that adds advertising, analytics, accounts, networking, cloud services, new permissions, or any data collection.
